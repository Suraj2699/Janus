Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Janus methodology.

Most apps are blank canvases that let you hoard tasks indefinitely. Janus is an opinionated execution system. It forces every task to belong to a strategic goal, clears your queue daily, and prevents multitasking, ensuring you work on what actually moves the needle rather than just "getting things done."
Yes. This is a core feature, not a bug. "Orphan" tasks are often busy work in disguise. By requiring a parent Goal or Project, Janus forces you to justify why a task exists before you commit to doing it. If it doesn't serve a goal, it shouldn't be on your list.
"Today's Focus" is a temporary, 24-hour list. You manually curate it each morning from your project backlogs. It separates planning (deciding what to do) from execution (doing it), so when you work, you execute the list without decision fatigue. Any unfinished tasks are returned to the backlog at midnight.
Absolutely. Use the "Ideas" inbox to capture fleeting thoughts or tasks that aren't ready for action. They stay safe in the background and won't clutter your daily view until you explicitly promote them to a Project.
No. Janus is strictly for solo execution. We intentionally omit team features, calendars, and habit trackers to keep the interface silent and focused. It works alongside your calendar, but it doesn't try to replace it.
